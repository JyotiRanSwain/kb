Manufacturing

Inside the production journey

Each bottle and jar passes through a defined sequence of steps before it's ready to deliver.

01

Water Treatment

Incoming water begins the treatment sequence.

02

Purification

Treated water is purified ahead of bottling.

03

Bottle Preparation

Bottles and jars are prepared for filling.

04

Filling

Purified water is filled into containers.

05

Capping

Filled containers are securely capped.

06

Labeling

Containers are labeled ahead of packaging.

07

Packaging

Products are packaged for despatch.

08

Quality Inspection

A final inspection precedes delivery.
